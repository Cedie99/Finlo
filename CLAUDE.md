# Installment Tracker - Problem Statement

## Overview

This application addresses the fragmented and confusing nature of modern consumer debt management across multiple payment methods (credit cards, BNPL services, and installment plans).

---

## The 10 Core Problems We Solve

### 1. "I didn't realize how much I owe in total"

**Problem:** People use multiple payment methods (credit card + BNPL + installments), but there's no single clear view.

**Why it happens:**

- BNPL splits payments → feels small individually
- Different due dates across platforms
- No unified dashboard

**Result:**

- Debt sneaks up
- Overcommitment (they think they can still afford more)

---

### 2. Missed or late payments

**Problem:** Users forget due dates or confuse schedules.

**Why it happens:**

- Installments have fixed schedules
- Credit cards have statement vs due date confusion
- BNPL apps each have their own reminders

**Result:**

- Late fees
- Penalties
- Lower credit score

---

### 3. "Minimum payment trap" (credit cards)

**Problem:** Users only pay the minimum amount due.

**Why it happens:**

- Minimum looks "safe"
- Lack of understanding of interest compounding

**Result:**

- Debt grows instead of shrinking
- Long-term interest becomes massive

---

### 4. Hidden fees & interest confusion

**Problem:** Users don't fully understand the cost of what they're buying.

**Why it happens:**

- "0% interest" promos with hidden conditions
- Late fees not obvious
- BNPL feels "free" but penalizes missed payments heavily

**Result:**

- Unexpected charges
- Distrust in financial systems

---

### 5. Overspending due to psychological effects

**Problem:** People spend more than they normally would.

**Why it happens:**

- BNPL reduces "pain of paying"
- Installments make expensive items feel cheap
- Credit cards delay consequences

**Result:**

- Impulse buying
- Stacking multiple obligations

---

### 6. Cash flow mismatch

**Problem:** Income timing doesn't match payment schedules.

**Why it happens:**

- Salary dates vs due dates misaligned
- Multiple installment schedules overlapping

**Result:**

- Short-term cash shortages
- Need to borrow again → debt cycle

---

### 7. Lack of prioritization

**Problem:** Users don't know which bill to pay first.

**Why it happens:**

- Different interest rates
- Different penalties
- No clear guidance

**Result:**

- Paying low-impact debts first
- High-interest debts grow faster

---

### 8. No forecasting (future stress blindness)

**Problem:** People don't see future financial commitments clearly.

**Why it happens:**

- No timeline visualization
- BNPL spreads payments into the future invisibly

**Result:**

- "Next month me will handle it" mindset
- Future months become overloaded

---

### 9. Fragmented platforms

**Problem:** Everything is scattered.

**Examples:**

- Credit card app
- BNPL app (e.g., Atome, BillEase)
- Bank app
- Manual tracking (or none)

**Result:**

- No holistic control
- High cognitive load

---

### 10. Emotional stress & anxiety

**Problem:** People avoid checking their finances.

**Why it happens:**

- Fear of seeing total debt
- Confusion
- Shame around spending

**Result:**

- Avoidance → worse situation
- Financial decisions become reactive, not planned

---

## How This App Addresses Each Problem

| Problem                 | Solution Feature                                        |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1. Total debt blindness | Unified dashboard showing all obligations               |
| 2. Missed payments      | Centralized calendar with notifications                 |
| 3. Minimum payment trap | Payoff projections + interest cost visibility           |
| 4. Hidden fees          | Transparent cost breakdowns                             |
| 5. Overspending         | "Safe to spend" calculator + "Can I afford it?" checker |
| 6. Cash flow mismatch   | Payday alignment tools + overlap detection              |
| 7. No prioritization    | Debt payoff race (highest interest first)               |
| 8. No forecasting       | Commitment density calendar + future view               |
| 9. Fragmented platforms | Single unified tracking hub                             |
| 10. Emotional stress    | Friendly UI, progress visualization, gamification       |
